## About VantaRoot

VantaRoot is an independent cybersecurity research initiative dedicated to wireless security, protocol analysis, infrastructure security and the investigation of vulnerabilities across interconnected technologies.

This repository serves as the central technical publication archive for VantaRoot, hosting research papers, experimental investigations, security assessments and advanced technical PDFs.

Our research examines the security of wireless communication technologies, the protocols that govern them, the weaknesses that affect their implementations and the defensive mechanisms required to improve their resilience.

VantaRoot approaches wireless security as a technical research discipline, combining protocol analysis, controlled experimentation, vulnerability investigation and practical security validation.

## Technical Publication Framework

Each VantaRoot publication aims to follow a consistent research structure:

1. Abstract.
2. Research objectives and scope.
3. Technical background.
4. Protocol and architecture analysis.
5. Experimental methodology.
6. Laboratory configuration.
7. Security testing and observations.
8. Vulnerability analysis.
9. Risk and impact assessment.
10. Defensive countermeasures.
11. Conclusions.
12. References.

## Research Principles

**Protocol-level understanding**

Investigate the underlying protocols and communication mechanisms rather than relying exclusively on automated tools.

**Controlled experimentation**

Conduct testing in authorised environments, isolated laboratories and controlled wireless research setups.

**Technical reproducibility**

Document research procedures, configurations, observations and evidence.

**Responsible disclosure**

Handle discovered vulnerabilities responsibly and coordinate disclosure with affected parties where appropriate.

**Defensive advancement**

Translate technical research into meaningful security recommendations and improved wireless resilience.
